Policy
Last updated:
May 20, 2026
1
.
Data Sovereignty
At Cynbit Technologies, we believe you should own your data. We only collect the minimum information necessary to provide our services—typically your name, email, and professional context when you reach out via our forms.
2
.
How We Use Information
Information provided via our inquiry forms or BitElevate applications is used strictly to communicate with you about your request. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties for marketing purposes.
3
.
Security Standards
We employ industry-standard encryption and optimistic architecture patterns to ensure that any data stored within our systems (such as blog comments or diary entries) is protected against unauthorized access.
4
.
Third-Party Services
We use trusted partners like Resend for email delivery and Prisma for database management. These partners are vetted for their own rigorous privacy standards and only process data as instructed by us.
5
.
Your Rights
You have the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you, or to request its permanent deletion from our records. Simply contact us at opportunities@cynbit.in to initiate a request.
Questions about your data?
Our technical lead is available to clarify any privacy concerns.
