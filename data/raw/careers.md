The Methodology
Our operating
model
.
We've ditched the traditional corporate noise for a high-fidelity execution framework that prioritizes deep work and technical excellence.
01
Doc-Led Culture
We value clear documentation over endless meetings. If it's not in the blueprint, it doesn't exist.
02
Asynchronous Syncs
We respect your flow state. Most communication happens asynchronously via specialized engineering hubs.
03
Optimistic Reviews
Peer reviews are collaborative, not critical. We aim for the best architectural outcome, together.

Iterative Sprints
We ship fast, but we architect for the long-term. Our cycles balance rapid delivery with technical rigor.
